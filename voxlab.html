Rules of capture

Low: morning voice, relaxed, no growl or fry — sustain it 3 seconds. High: use head voice, not a scream; if your throat clenches, that note doesn't count yet. Re-test after two weeks of practice — range grows at the edges with consistent, gentle work.

Siren Glide

Slide from your lowest to highest note and back on "ng" or "oo" — one unbroken sound. Watch the trail on the Tuner tab while you do it: your goal is a smooth curve with no gaps. Gaps reveal your register break; work slowly through that zone daily and it seals.

Melody Trainer — from your tab to your voice

Enter the vocal melody as notes (D4 E4 F#4 A4) or read it straight off tablature with string + fret. VOXLAB saves it to this song, plays it back, then makes you land every note before moving on. Or skip the typing: photograph the tab and let AI transcribe it.

Outside of Claude, transcription needs your own API key from console.anthropic.com. It's stored only in this browser and sent only to Anthropic.

Octave notes: guitar sounds an octave lower than written, and your singing register may differ from the strings you tabbed. If playback sounds right but singing feels wrong, hit Octave −/+ to shift the whole line into your range.
